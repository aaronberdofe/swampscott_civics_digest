#### **16:30 – 46:01 | Agenda Item 6: Public Hearing – Citizens' Petition by Tom Bogacz**
* **16:34 | Opening:** **Bill Quinn** opens the public hearing for the drafting zoning amendment for a citizens' petition submitted by Tom Bogacz.
* **17:16 – 20:45 | Petitioner Presentation:** **Tom Bogacz** (25 Ingles Terrace, co-owner of Terpene Journey in Vinnin Square) presents his proposed amendment (*"Article to Align Medical Marijuana Zoning with State Law to Expand Local Retail Options"*):
  * Historically, Massachusetts required medical marijuana operators to be vertically integrated (growing, manufacturing, and retail), favoring large out-of-state companies. Adult-use/recreational cannabis allowed horizontal integration (standalone retail stores).
  * In the spring, the state changed its law to allow horizontal integration for medical marijuana, permitting standalone retail stores like his to serve medical patients.
  * Swampscott's current bylaws only allow medical marijuana sales in the Industrial district, while both existing recreational dispensaries are in Business districts. His petition proposes allowing medical sales wherever recreational retail sales are allowed.
  * **Buffer Zone Note:** Existing medical marijuana bylaws maintain a 1,000-foot buffer from elementary, middle, and high schools and public libraries. Bogacz states his competitor down the street is a few feet shy of 1,000 feet from the public library (clarified at **21:27**), meaning only his store would qualify for medical sales under the 1,000-foot rule unless Town Meeting lowers the buffer (e.g., to 500 feet) following neighbor feedback.

* **20:46 – 41:28 | Board Discussion & Q&A:**
  * **Size Thresholds (Lines 50–52):** Screen-sharing the redline amendment (**20:10**, **22:10**), **Ariane Purdy** and **Jer Jurma** note that proposed Line 51 (facilities containing 2,000 to 10,000 sq. ft. of gross floor area) would be allowed by right (`Y`) in districts B-3 and B-4, and by Special Permit (`SP`) in B-1 and B-2. Bogacz confirms his current store is 1,411 sq. ft. and says he copied the square-footage tiers from existing general retail bylaws so existing businesses wouldn't need a special permit unless general retail required one.
  * **Zoning Districts:** **Christa** shares the MassGIS zoning map (**27:00**, **35:49**), confirming Vinnin Square is split between B-3 and B-4, while the other dispensary (corner of New Ocean) is in B-1.
  * **Town Counsel Review:** **Christa** reports (**29:27**) that KP Law's marijuana specialist reviewed the draft and found it structurally sound from a zoning standpoint, only questioning why the 1,000-foot buffer was left unchanged.
  * **Dispensary Cap & Operations:** Board members and Bogacz confirm Swampscott's cap of two cannabis dispensaries (tied to state law and liquor license percentages) is separate from zoning and would not be overridden. Bogacz outlines operational differences for medical sales: medical purchases are tax-exempt (versus the 3% local tax on recreational), require a dedicated checkout line, include higher edible dosage limits (up to 1,000 mg vs. 100 mg), and require state tracking of medical patient cards. He estimates medical sales might draw 50 to 150–200 customers per day.

* **41:58 – 42:08 | Closing the Public Hearing:**
  * **Motion:** Close the public hearing.
    * **Moved by:** Jer Jurma (**42:05**)
    * **Seconded by:** Ariane Purdy (**42:06**)
  * **Outcome:** Public hearing closed.

* **42:09 – 45:56 | Board Deliberation & Recommendation Vote:**
  * **Ariane Purdy** and **Jer Jurma** express support for the overall alignment and clearer definitions, but recommend changing B-3 and B-4 on Line 51 (2,000–10,000 sq. ft.) from by right (`Y`) to Special Permit (`SP`) to ensure review for any future large-scale dispensary.
  * **Tom Bogacz** agrees with the change. **Bill Quinn** and **Christa** confirm the amendment cannot be altered prior to Town Meeting because it was already advertised as printed, so the modification must be made as a floor amendment at Town Meeting, backed by the Planning Board's recommendation.
  * **Motion:** Recommend **favorable action** (endorsement) at Town Meeting, with the suggested modification for districts B-3 and B-4 (on Line 51) to require a Special Permit.
    * **Moved by:** Jer Jurma (**45:37**)
    * **Seconded by:** Ariane Purdy (**45:50**)
  * **Vote Outcome:** Passed **3–0** (All in favor: *"Aye"*).
