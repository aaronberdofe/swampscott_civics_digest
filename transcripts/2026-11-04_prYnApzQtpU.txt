Here is the detailed chronological record of the Swampscott Select Board meeting held on November 4, 2026.

### **Meeting Information**
*   **Board Members Present:** Katie Phelan (Chair), Danielle Leonard, Wayne Spritz, Ted Dooley.
*   **Town Administrator:** Nick Connors.

---

### **Chronological Meeting Record**

**06:17 – Call to Order**
*   Chair Katie Phelan officially calls the meeting to order.

**06:30 – Pledge of Allegiance**
*   The Board and attendees recite the Pledge of Allegiance.

**06:55 – Town Administrator’s Report (Nick Connors)**
*   **Election Recognition:** Connors thanks the registrar, election workers, volunteers, and high school staff for their support during the recent election. Special thanks provided to Maria, Dave Aldridge, the Bagel Bus, and Reggie Pagen.
*   **Operations:** Connors highlights successful work by Mike Bryson and Katie DuPont. Plans to review school operations and coordination with the police are ongoing.
*   **Public Safety:** Chief Koseda noted that several officers will be receiving a Star Award for their response to an incident in Vinnin Square.
*   **Performing Arts Center:** The Swampscott Center for Performing Arts is currently behind on rent. Connors notes they are working on a plan for catch-up payments.
*   **Track Project:** A donation from Big Blue Bargains has been received to support the track project.
*   **Veteran’s Banners:** Installation of banners is underway in coordination with the Military Friends Foundation.
*   **Upcoming Meetings:** A task force presentation regarding the Community Life Center is scheduled for the following Tuesday at 6:30 PM.
*   **Veteran’s Day:** Announcement of upcoming events.

**12:56 – Public Comment**
*   **Patrick McCarthy (87 Essex St):** Questions the safety assessment of Burrill St. He expresses concern regarding traffic congestion, parking, and safety for the VFW and neighbors.
*   **Correspondence:** Katie Phelan reads an email from Lindsay Bennett regarding parking and safety concerns at 89 Burrill St.
*   **Tom Bogaz (25 Ingalls Terrace):** Discusses issues with the voting machine during the election, street parking, trash pickup, and the distinction between public and private property regarding placement of trash/recycling bins.

**16:50 – Agenda Item 9: Zoning Bylaw Changes & Articles**
*   The Board reviews various articles.
*   **Article 13:** Discussion on the Burrill Center Senior Center Plan development district.
*   **Article 14:** Discussion on building height measurement illustration.

**26:45 – Article 5: Appropriate Funds for Special Ed Reserve**
*   **Discussion:** The Board discusses the financial aspect of the special education reserve fund.
*   **Motion:** A motion is made to recommend Article 5 for approval.
*   **Second:** The motion is seconded.
*   **Vote:** Unanimously approved (All in favor).

**27:30 – Parking Restrictions/Emergency Vehicle Access**
*   The Board discusses parking on specific streets (Ingalls Terrace, Cedar Hill Terrace, Burrill St, etc.).
*   **Safety Assessment:** Discussion regarding traffic patterns and the need for "Resident Only" parking signs to ensure emergency vehicle access.
*   **Review:** The Board examines maps and diagrams of proposed parking changes.

**146:00 – Article 12: Accessory Outdoor Seating**
*   **Discussion:** The Board reviews the accessory outdoor seating bylaw.
*   **Motion:** A motion is made to recommend Article 12 for approval.
*   **Second:** The motion is seconded.
*   **Vote:** Unanimously approved (All in favor).

**17:28 – Financial Articles**
*   Review of General Fund Expenditures - Budget-to-Actuals.
*   Topics: Personnel savings (vacancies), property liability insurance, state assessments, and health insurance.
*   Discussion of the "General Fund Revenue" trends.
*   Specific mention of Meals Tax receipts (up 23%) and Rooms Tax receipts (up 17%).

**18:30 – Consent Agenda**
*   **Discussion:** A request for a one-day change to liquor license hours to allow a 9:00 AM start time (to accommodate a Patriots game/Town Meeting conflict).
*   **Motion:** Motion to approve the consent agenda.
*   **Second:** Seconded.
*   **Vote:** Unanimously approved (All in favor).

**18:40 – New & Old Business**
*   The Board continues discussion on various zoning articles and potential changes to bylaws (Articles 1-15).
*   Discussion on "Utility Box Mural Policy."
*   Mention of a specific draft policy regarding utility box murals. The Board agrees to bring this back at a future meeting.

**19:00 – Meeting Adjournment**
*   The meeting is adjourned.
